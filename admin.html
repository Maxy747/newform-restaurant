    Settings

    Cash on delivery

    Delivery orders of ₹799 or more (before delivery charge) can pay cash on delivery. Takeaway and dine-in can always pay at the counter.

    Home delivery

    Turn off when no delivery staff are available. Customers can still order takeaway and dine in; the server refuses new delivery orders while this is off.

    New-order alerts on this device

    The order sound is on by default (speaker icon at the top). Desktop notifications also show when this tab is in the background.

    Invoices

    When on, each order gets the next invoice number as soon as it is both completed and paid. Numbers restart every financial year (April). Issued invoices can't be edited or deleted. To correct one, issue a credit note.
